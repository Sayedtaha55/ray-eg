# Product Definition

## Product Type

Business Operating Network (BON)

---

## The Product Is

✔ Business Platform

✔ ERP Platform

✔ Business Graph

✔ Marketplace Platform

✔ Booking Platform

✔ Communication Platform

✔ Analytics Platform

✔ AI Platform

---

## The Product Is NOT

❌ Traditional ERP

❌ Traditional CRM

❌ Simple Marketplace

❌ Google Maps Clone

❌ Booking Clone

## Product Goals

Reduce operational costs.

Increase customer acquisition.

Increase collaboration between businesses.

Create a business ecosystem.

Build network effects.

Enable AI-powered automation.

## North Star

Every feature should strengthen at least one of these:

Business Operations

Business Connectivity

Customer Journey

Business Intelligence

Automation

Revenue Growth
